Lvl 1 – Intro to 3D Printing Training

Location: The Hatchery Makerspace – 245 Beacon St., Room 203

Information: Want to learn the basics of 3D printing? Our Level 1 FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) training does just that! Learn to operate our Bambu A1 Mini 3D printers and begin printing using plastic filament (provided by The Hatchery).

After completing this training and earning 3 stamps, users can then sign up for Level 2 trainings:

  • Lvl 2 Multi-Material 3D Printing – trains you to use the multi-filament Bambu FDM printers.
  • Lvl 2 High-Detail 3D Printing – trains you to use the SLA resin printers.

The Lvl 3 trainings include Photo-Realistic 3D Printing with the Stratasys J55 full-color PolyJet printer.

Prerequisite: Makerspace Orientation Required – bit.ly/hatcheryorientation

Reserve Training
Lvl 2 – High-Detail Resin 3D Printing Training

Location: The Hatchery Prototyping Studio – 245 Beacon St., Room 301

Information: SLA printing is a resin-based 3D printing process! Create highly-detailed prints with our FormLabs and Prusa SLA printers. Users will learn how to safely handle liquid resin, how to run the printer, and correct after-care for SLA prints.

Prerequisites:

  • 3D Printing Level 1 Training – Prusa Mini (3 stamps)
  • Ultimaker and Bambu (3 stamps)
Reserve Training
Lvl 2 – Multi-Material 3D Printing Training

Location: The Hatchery Prototyping Studio – 245 Beacon St., Room 301

Information: Take FDM printing to the next level! Our Bambu Labs 3D printers are capable of larger 3D prints, printing in multiple colors, and significantly faster printing too.

Prerequisites:

  • Ultimaker and Bambu (3 stamps)

Lvl 3 – Photo-Realistic 3D Printing Training

Location: The Hatchery Prototyping Studio – 245 Beacon St., Room 301

Information: Want to bring your photorealistic 3D models to life? Our Level 3 Stratasys (Photo-Realistic) Printer training does just that! Learn to operate the Stratasys 3D Printer and create hyperrealistic objects. In this training, you will learn how to 3D scan something, edit your scan in Blender, and 3D print it on the Stratasys PolyJet printer.
