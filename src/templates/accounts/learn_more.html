Getting Started

Orientation

The Hatchery Makerspaces are for everyone. The first step in joining our maker community is taking the orientation. Once you complete our online safety orientation, you can access tool trainings, equipment, and other events inside The Hatchery Makerspaces.

This self-paced experience will take approximately 25 minutes to complete. You can take as long as you need. To begin the orientation, click “Get Started!” below and use your Boston College credentials. Once logged in, the course content will appear below.

After finishing the online orientation, you will unlock the ability to enroll in hands-on training sessions for specific tools. Orientation completion is required before booking any equipment training.

Get Started!

Trainings

Tool Trainings, led by Hatchery Student Team Members, educate users on how to safely operate our tools and equipment, all while making a product of their own.

Trainings are scheduled through the BC Makerspace calendar once your orientation status is verified. Choose the tool you want to learn, select an available session, and confirm your spot using your BC credentials.

If you don’t see a time that works for you, email hatchery@bc.edu with your availability and the machine you’d like to access, and a team member will help coordinate a session.

Mission Statement

In the spirit of 'Think to Make for Impact,' being able to iterate, visualize, refine, and communicate creative design ideas using prototypes is an essential skill set for our students entering the future workforce. The mission of the Boston College (BC) Makerspaces (The Hatchery) is to enable these transformative capabilities, irrespective of background or discipline, by providing an equitable, accessible, and well-equipped community-oriented space for all. Wherever you are on your making and design journey, The Hatchery Makerspaces at Boston College encourages all members of the community to let their ideas fly.

Course Collaboration

Interested in incorporating The Hatchery into your credit-bearing course?

Course Collaboration Form
Introduction to Product Design
Students in this course utilized The Hatchery's 3D printing facilities and laser cutters to create functional prototypes of their product designs. They worked on developing ergonomic phone cases and sustainable packaging solutions, bringing their CAD models to life through hands-on making.
"The Hatchery transformed our classroom experience. Students were able to see their designs come to life in ways that weren't possible before. The prototyping process became an integral part of their learning journey, and the staff's support was invaluable." - Professor Sarah Chen
Engineering Design Studio
Engineering students collaborated at The Hatchery to build working prototypes of assistive devices for individuals with disabilities. They used the woodworking tools, electronics equipment, and fabrication resources to create functional prototypes that were tested with community partners.
"The Hatchery provided our students with the tools and space to think beyond theory. Watching them iterate on their designs, test prototypes, and refine their solutions in real-time was incredible. This hands-on experience is exactly what prepares them for industry." - Professor Michael Rodriguez
Digital Fabrication in Art
Art students explored the intersection of traditional art and digital fabrication, using The Hatchery's CNC machines and vinyl cutters to create large-scale installations. They experimented with materials and techniques to produce interactive art pieces that were displayed in campus galleries.
"The Hatchery opened up entirely new possibilities for artistic expression. Our students pushed boundaries and created work that wouldn't have been possible without access to these tools. The collaborative atmosphere fostered incredible creativity and innovation." - Professor Emily Watson

Event Collaboration

Planning an event with your student organization? Partner with The Hatchery!

Event Collaboration Form
Asian Caucus
Asian Caucus organized a cultural celebration event at The Hatchery, where members created traditional-inspired decorations and interactive displays using the makerspace's tools. They designed and fabricated lanterns, banners, and cultural artifacts that enhanced their event's atmosphere.
"The Hatchery helped us bring our vision to life in ways we never imagined. The staff was incredibly supportive, and having access to professional tools allowed us to create decorations that truly represented our culture. Our event was more meaningful because of the personal touch we were able to add." - Asian Caucus Event Coordinator
Center for Student Wellness
The Center for Student Wellness hosted a mindfulness and creativity workshop at The Hatchery, where students created personalized stress-relief tools and meditation aids. Participants used the space to craft custom fidget devices, aromatherapy diffusers, and calming visual art pieces.
"Combining wellness practices with hands-on making was transformative. Students found the process of creating something with their hands to be incredibly therapeutic. The Hatchery provided the perfect environment for this unique approach to self-care and mental health awareness." - Center for Student Wellness Director
The Golden Eagles Dance Team
The Golden Eagles Dance Team collaborated with The Hatchery to design and fabricate custom props and stage elements for their annual performance. They created lightweight, durable props including illuminated accessories and modular set pieces that enhanced their choreography.
"Working with The Hatchery elevated our performance to a whole new level. The props we created were not only beautiful but perfectly suited to our choreography. The ability to prototype and refine our designs ensured everything worked flawlessly on stage. It was a game-changer for our team." - Golden Eagles Dance Team Captain
